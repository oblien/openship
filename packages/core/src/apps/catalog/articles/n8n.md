## Turn repeated work into a workflow

n8n connects applications and APIs through a visual workflow editor. Use it for internal automations, data movement, notifications, and integrations.

The template deploys the workflow service on your chosen server. Open the editor after installation, configure your integrations, and choose the event that starts the workflow.

## Make one workflow reliable first

Check connected-service credentials and test representative inputs before using a workflow for unattended work. Review execution results and protect the service's persistent data.

The [n8n documentation](https://docs.n8n.io/) covers deployment requirements, integrations, and upstream licensing.
