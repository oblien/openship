## Understand your website traffic

Umami provides website analytics without a cookie-based tracker. Use its dashboard to understand pageviews, referrers, and visitor activity.

The template runs the application with its Postgres database. Add your website in Umami, then place the tracking script in that site's frontend. The service and data stay on your chosen infrastructure.

## Start with one website

Review the initial account settings, register the website, and copy its tracking snippet. Verify that visits appear before rolling the script out more widely.

The [Umami documentation](https://umami.is/docs) covers tracking and dashboard configuration. Newer upstream features may require an update from the template's installed release.
