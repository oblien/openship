Running the Openship control plane yourself remains free. **Openship Manage** pays

**Openship Cloud** includes the compute and its management.

## Your infrastructure: Openship Manage

Manage costs **$5/month per workspace (organization)** and includes two connected
servers. Each additional server is **$3/month**. Connect a server using direct SSH
with a password or an uploaded private key. Your apps, builds, volumes and edge
routes run on that server; this plan provides no Cloud compute or storage.

Open **Billing → Your infrastructure** to subscribe, review a prorated increase,
schedule a smaller allowance at renewal, cancel a pending checkout, or cancel and
resume renewal. Payment is verified by the server before access is granted.
Disconnect unused servers before reducing the allowance.

A paid Cloud Space includes the base two-server allowance once per organization;
Cloud Spaces do not consume these slots. Existing Manage base charges end at
renewal after Cloud coverage activates. Extra-server charges keep their saved
terms. If Cloud coverage ends, a discounted Manage renewal stops rather than
automatically increasing its price. The already-paid period remains available;
choose a full Manage subscription after it ends.

Canceling or expiring Manage stops hosted management after the paid period. It
does **not** stop or delete customer apps or data. Disconnecting a server keeps
its workloads by default; destructive removal remains an explicit separate choice.
Product support is included; dedicated operations and hardware have separate terms.

For physical dedicated hardware, request a quote from the pricing page. Hardware,
region and support terms must be confirmed before purchase.

## Monthly Cloud Spaces

Each [managed Cloud server](/docs/guides/cloud-workspaces) has its own subscription.
A monthly plan covers the purchased CPU, RAM and disk for the full paid period.
There is **no second compute-credit allowance** and no compute top-up to keep a
paid monthly server running. CPU is shared capacity, not exclusive physical cores.

| Plan | Per month | vCPU | RAM | Disk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $20 | 2 | 8 GiB | 128 GiB |
| Pro | $39 | 4 | 16 GiB | 250 GiB |
| Scale | $99 | 8 | 32 GiB | 600 GiB |

Existing $5 Hobby subscriptions keep their saved renewal price and capacity.
That offer is closed to new purchases; new monthly Cloud plans start at $20.

The [pricing page](/pricing) and `GET /api/billing/plans` publish the current offers.
Choose **Custom** to set CPU, RAM and disk and review a monthly quote before
checkout. Custom uses the same managed-server subscription and payment flow.
A server can have at most 12 vCPU. Enterprise terms are available through sales.
An account without a plan has no included Cloud compute.

Openship owns retail prices. Oblien verifies resource availability, payment and
paid-through coverage. Renewals retain saved terms; changes to the public catalog
do not alter a purchased contract. Existing metered subscriptions are not
converted automatically and continue to show their saved allowance in Billing.

## Sharing your server

Apps and source builds share the server's CPU, RAM and disk. A project can use the
full available capacity; the operating system, builds and other projects also
need room. Container limits do not reserve another VM or disk. Image-only installs
do not create a source builder, and undeployed drafts do not use service slots.
There is no monthly build-time cap on these monthly offers.

The server page separates purchased capacity from measured CPU, memory and disk
usage, including a project disk breakdown. An unknown measurement stays unknown.
A provisioned disk size is not the amount of data stored on it. Add another
subscribed server when you need a separate resource pool.

Every server plan includes unlimited team members without a per-seat charge.

## Coverage and separate services

**Overview** shows the billing mode, coverage status and paid-through date.
**Usage** shows recorded resource usage; monthly servers show CPU time instead
of compute-credit spend. A recorded usage comparison uses the contract's saved
PAYG rates. It is not a promised cash saving or a new charge for monthly compute.

Monthly servers include managed internet transfer. Billing shows the provider's
available allowance (or **Unlimited**) and usage for the current period. This
covers Git clones, package downloads and other managed internet traffic; public
app-route traffic and backups are separate. Exhausting transfer cannot end a
confirmed paid month of CPU and RAM.

The **Prepaid PAYG** pricing preview shows Openship resource tiers separately
from credit packages. **Tier 1**, **Tier 2** and **Tier 3** describe the combined
CPU, RAM, storage and server limits for a PAYG pool. The tier tabs show the credit
purchase threshold and the pool's capacity. These numbers stay fixed while you
configure a server. Credit
purchases unlock tiers; spending credits does not lower an unlocked tier.
Selecting a tier or package in the preview does not grant it.

Customize CPU, RAM and storage to estimate one server's hourly cost at full CPU
usage. Servers share the tier's pool allowance. If a configuration exceeds the selected pool,
the preview keeps your inputs and offers a larger tier that fits when available.
The credit card shows which tier the selected package would unlock. Resource
rates and the credit conversion come from the provider's published catalog:
active CPU time, reserved RAM time, and retained provisioned storage.

CPU time is measured in **vCPU-hours**, separately from elapsed time online.
Four allocated vCPUs averaging 25% activity consume one vCPU-hour in one elapsed
hour. Two such hosts consume two vCPU-hours during that same hour. The estimator
shows the configured server's hourly resource usage at full CPU activity. Lower CPU
activity reduces CPU usage; reserved RAM and storage continue to accrue usage.
The estimate leads with dollars per hour and the equivalent credit deduction,
with a visible CPU, RAM and storage breakdown. Open **Balance estimate** for the duration range from
100% CPU activity to idle CPU, including RAM and storage at both ends. These are
usage scenarios, not a guaranteed number of days or a required monthly deposit.

PAYG estimates use the published resource rates throughout the displayed period.
Balance duration is prepaid funds divided by usage cost; monthly host prices
do not set a ceiling on the estimate. Optional proxy transfer, taxes and storage
retained after compute ends are separate.
Purchases are not available yet. Selecting PAYG does not switch an existing
monthly server, buy credits or debit a wallet.

## Managing your plan

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Current plan, resource coverage, billing period and renewal controls. |
| **Usage** | Recorded usage over the selected period. |
| **Plans** | Compare upgrades, review other plans, or customize resources. |
| **Top-ups** | Available only for eligible saved metered subscriptions. |
| **Payments** | Unfinished checkouts, plus invoices and payment methods through the selected server's Stripe portal. |

Choose the managed server to inspect its own billing. **Get server** starts a
separate subscription; it does not replace the selected server's plan. New
customers see plan selection without empty usage or payment-history tabs.

Choose **Review change** to see Oblien's amount due now, prorated adjustment,
effective date and the projects that may restart. Upgrades apply after confirmed
payment. Resource reductions and billing-mode changes wait for renewal, even
when their retail price is higher. The returned preview is authoritative.
Usage and already purchased top-ups remain attached to their existing terms.

Pending changes stay visible in the subscription card. Complete payment there,
or cancel a pending change when allowed. Cancel a pending change before stopping
or resuming renewal. Retries use the same financial operation.

After payment, the shared server worker applies the approved resources and
restarts affected projects. If the server changed since review, **Apply plan
capacity** asks for a fresh restart confirmation. An existing disk can grow but
cannot shrink in place. Choose Custom to keep the disk while reducing CPU or RAM,
or migrate to a smaller server.

First subscriptions use hosted checkout. The return page checks the specific
payment and provider fulfillment; a successful browser redirect does not grant
coverage. Pending, refunded and failed payments remain distinct.
Choosing a preset on the public Pricing page opens Cloud checkout for that plan
after sign-in. You do not need to select it again. If an offer is no longer
available, Cloud shows the current plans instead. Refreshing or retrying that
purchase reuses its server and payment attempt.

If a checkout was left open, **Pending payments** in the Billing header lets you
resume its original payment or cancel an unpaid checkout. Cloud uses the provider’s
checkout cancellation capability, with capacity-quote cancellation retained for
older provider deployments.
This remains available before a server has a paid plan. A pending cancellation
keeps the purchase tracked until confirmed; a payment that completes meanwhile
opens its verification status instead of another checkout. Confirmed cancellation
or expiry lets you choose a new plan and remove an unused, unpaid server.
If cancellation cannot be verified, refresh the payment status. If this payment
has no supported cancellation, complete it or wait for expiry before selecting
another plan; removing its local record would leave its payment link active.

## Cancellation and retained storage

Canceling renewal keeps the current paid period. You can resume renewal before
it ends. Compute stops after coverage expires unless renewal is confirmed;
a manual suspension can also stop a server during a paid period.

Cancellation does not delete data. The provider's initial tariff retains disks
for at least 30 days with no automatic deletion. Retained provisioned storage
is billed at the saved rate after compute coverage ends, until those disks are
actually deleted. The minimum retention review date does not stop those charges.
Billing shows the contract's retention terms and any amount due. Contact
[support](/support) if a storage payment needs attention.

## Credit warnings and recovery

Credit warnings apply to saved metered subscriptions, not monthly capacity
contracts. When applicable, a compact notice in the bottom corner identifies
the server and can be opened or dismissed. Top-ups increase that subscription's
metered allowance without increasing its CPU, RAM or disk limits.

A top-up cannot restart an ended or past-due subscription. Restore its payment
first. Checkout waits for provider confirmation before showing new credits.
The warning refreshes when you return to the dashboard and every minute while
visible. Notifications follow the user's preferences; email needs a verified
address and a working mail connection.

See the [billing API](/docs/api/billing) for the operations behind these screens.
