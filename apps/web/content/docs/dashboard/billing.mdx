<Callout title="Openship Cloud only">
The Billing area appears only on **Openship Cloud**. A self-hosted install has
nothing to bill, so the screen doesn't exist there.
</Callout>

## Self-hosted has no billing

A plain **self-hosted** Openship has nothing to pay and shows no billing data —
the Billing area simply doesn't appear. You only pay for your own infrastructure.

## Openship Cloud

On Cloud, **Billing** is where you watch usage, change plan, and pull invoices.
Plans, prices, and per-plan limits are published on the
[pricing page](/pricing); the header strip on this screen shows how much of your
plan's credits, bandwidth, build minutes, and capacity you've used in the current
period.

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Current plan, billing period, and credit balance. |
| **Usage** | Metered usage over the period, charted. |
| **Plans** | Upgrade or downgrade — starts a Stripe Checkout session. |
| **Top-ups** | One-shot credit packs (coming to the dashboard). |
| **Payment Method** | Opens the Stripe portal to manage cards. |
| **Invoices** | Opens the Stripe portal to download invoices. |

Cancelling from **Plans** flips the subscription to end at the close of the
period you've already paid for; the organisation then drops back to the free
tier. Nothing is deleted.

For the endpoints behind this screen, see the [billing API](/docs/api/billing).
